## Operational distinctions

An overlap violation is structural only if the logging mechanism has zero probability on required target support. No observed matching row can also arise by chance under positive support. Both should stop an unjustified confident answer, but their scientific interpretation and remedies differ.

A pointwise 95% confidence interval is a repeated-sampling procedure under assumptions. It is not a 95% posterior probability about a realized interval, and selecting a favorable policy or subgroup after inspecting many intervals changes the inferential problem.

The sensitivity parameter is an assumption about hidden assignment odds. Widening Gamma examines a nested set of possible mechanisms. It does not estimate the actual confounder. Confidence in an estimated endpoint and the width of the identification region are separate uncertainties.
