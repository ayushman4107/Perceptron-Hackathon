# Prioritized remediation plan

This plan separates completed manuscript repairs from work required before claiming a validated system. No critical defect in the central IPS/DR identities or restricted binary sensitivity algebra was established. Major gaps remain in inference, experiments, and proposed extensions.

| Priority | Issue and status | Concrete next step | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1: central correctness | No established critical identity error; unsupported safety claims would be critical if made | Keep policy revision explicitly a proposal. Do not label a mixture safe merely because it is normalized or closer to the logger. | Every safety claim names a specific theorem, applicable assumptions, and implementation; otherwise label it heuristic. |
| 2: inference assumptions | M01, M03, M04: text corrected; deployment assumptions unverified | Predeclare IID versus clustered/adaptive sampling, fixed target, moment regime, and nuisance-training protocol. Record whether propensities are known design probabilities or estimates. | A reproducible data contract and estimator specification match the selected inferential method. |
| 2: sensitivity inference | M07, M08: population derivation verified; endpoint confidence procedure absent | Implement a justified finite-stratum simultaneous confidence region for observed cell probabilities, then optimize bounds over that region; specify allocation across strata, arms, and Gamma values. Treat empty cells explicitly. | Coverage analysis applies to binary rewards and boundary cells; simulation tests cover rare outcomes and empty treatment cells. Do not reuse a continuous-density theorem unchanged. |
| 2: selection | M11: citation corrected; no selection-adjusted evaluation | Freeze candidate policies and use an independent final evaluation set; alternatively justify a simultaneous procedure for the prespecified candidate family. | Final estimates do not reuse evaluation outcomes to select policy or thresholds without correction. |
| 3: finite-sample validity | M12: empirical undercoverage established | Compare conventional Wald intervals with a justified bounded-score procedure where overlap bounds are known. Include a deliberately conservative/abstaining reference. | Report empirical coverage, interval width, decision rate and false-improvement rate across independent seeds, with Monte Carlo SE; no arbitrary ESS cutoff advertised as a guarantee. |
| 3: generator specification | M09: independent-noise requirement added; general logistic family remains a proposal | Freeze coefficients, joint law of measured/hidden features, time effects, action eligibility, potential-reward noise and seed schedule for each proposed stress test. | Oracle truth computed independently of OPE code; evaluator-only hidden variables never passed to the deployable estimator. |
| 3: scientific claim | M06: “proof” changed to empirical verification | Extend beyond the small tabular simulator to multiple context dimensions, misspecified learners, drift, clustered users and adaptive logging as separate designs. | Results are labeled by the sampling regime each method supports; unsupported regimes are reported as stress tests, not validated inference. |
| 3: revision contribution | M13: normalization/value identity established; learning rule and guarantees absent | Specify alpha(x), support restrictions, objective, tuning, baseline and selection split. | 4: baselines and real data | Not executed | Run a documented OBD subset with its evaluation protocol and eligibility/position treatment. | 4: novelty | Application integration is defensible; algorithmic novelty not established | Compare to Su et al. estimator selection, PAS-IF, SPIBB where applicable, and sensitivity-aware policy work. | 5: reproducibility | Audit scripts/results supplied; product unavailable | Pin the eventual runtime, capture dataset checksums, splits, model settings and immutable result manifests. | 6: presentation | Revised Markdown and diff supplied | Once empirical scope is frozen, shorten the dossier into the required submission format and retain derivations as supplement. | Round-one plan satisfies the PDF's 600–900-word technical-plan format; prototype claims are reserved for implemented work. |

The remaining major issues are thus addressed either by explicit scope restrictions (already applied) or by concrete future experiments/procedures above. This audit does not silently implement a different research method to close them. M02's calibration claim is corrected in text; calibration can still be useful as a diagnostic, but it must not gate validity of known-propensity IPS/DR as a necessary mathematical condition.

Suggested order for a hackathon prototype: freeze the data contract and estimand; implement the verified estimator interfaces; build the synthetic truth evaluator; reproduce the negative coverage results; add a transparent decision/abstention rule; then attempt real-data replication. Sensitivity confidence regions and learned policy revision should remain labeled stretch work until separately validated.
