## What this comparison does and does not establish

The direct equality between C1 and min_q[V_q(pi)-V_q(pi0)] is independently derived in the technical specification. It does not require finding a paper with our exact descriptive phrase. The source comparisons establish close antecedents; they do not claim every published method has identical assumptions or finite-sample behavior.

The implemented `robust_reference` is the exact scalar solution for OUR rectangular objective. It is not represented as a full reproduction of L1, L2, or L3. This distinction prevents both overstating implementation scope and defeating an artificially weak baseline. If a future candidate adds coupled smoothness constraints, it must use an appropriate LP baseline and test sensitivity to the added assumptions.

No claim is made that every 2026 paper was retrieved. Some potentially relevant recent work was inspected only at the abstract level and is identified accordingly. Website crawl dates were not treated as publication dates. Search results from secondary summaries were used only to locate primary sources; they are not scientific evidence for the conclusions here.
